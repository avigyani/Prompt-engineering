About

Prompt Engineering, in plain words.

People no longer learn to code first. They learn to instruct AI — and the people who write the best prompts get the best work out of every AI tool, in minutes instead of hours. This site teaches that skill.

What this site is

Prompt engineering is the skill of writing clear, structured instructions for AI tools such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini so they produce exactly the result you want. A good prompt replaces hours of manual work and, increasingly, replaces writing code from scratch. You do not need to know coding to learn it — it is a language skill, not a programming skill.

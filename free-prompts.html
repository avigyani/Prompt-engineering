Product photo that sells

Professional product photograph of [product name], centred on a clean [colour] background with soft natural window light from the left. Subtle shadow under the product, no text, no watermark, no people. Shot on a 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, high-end e-commerce style, 4K detail.
Best for: Midjourney, DALL·E, Firefly

Logo concept generator

Minimal modern logo for a business called [business name] that sells [what you sell]. One simple symbol plus the name in clean sans-serif type. Style: [elegant / playful / bold]. Colours: [two colours]. Flat vector style, no gradients, no shadows, works in a single colour on a dark background. Give me 3 different concepts and explain each in one line.
Best for: ChatGPT, Claude, Ideogram

YouTube thumbnail that gets clicked

Bold YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, for a video titled "[video title]". One expressive close-up face on the right, large readable emotion, dramatic rim light. Left side: empty dark space for text. Colours: high contrast, [two accent colours]. No text in the image itself. Style: clean, punchy, professional — not cluttered.

Book cover concept

Book cover for a [genre] book titled "[title]" by [author]. Mood: [dark and mysterious / bright and hopeful]. 60-second reel script

Write a 60-second Instagram Reel script about [topic]. Structure: 0–3s hook that creates curiosity, 3–40s three quick points with one example each, 40–55s the common mistake people make, 55–60s call to action: [follow / comment a word / visit link]. Tone: [friendly and direct]. Write the on-screen text and the spoken lines separately.
Best for: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini

YouTube video outline

Create a detailed outline for a [length] YouTube video titled "[title]" for an audience of [who they are]. Include: a hook for the first 30 seconds, 4 main sections with 2 bullet points each, one story or example per section, and a closing that asks viewers to [subscribe / comment / try it]. Suggest 3 thumbnail ideas at the end.
Best for: ChatGPT, Claude

Product demo video script

Write a 90-second product demo script for [product]. The viewer has [problem]. Show the product solving it in 3 scenes: Scene 1 the problem in real life, Scene 2 the product in use with one feature per shot, Scene 3 the result and the price. End with: "[exact call to action]". Keep sentences short. No hype words like "amazing" or "revolutionary".
Best for: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini

Storyboard for a short ad

For each shot give: shot type (wide/close-up/overhead), what is on screen, the spoken line or on-screen text, and the music or sound. The ad should make the viewer feel [emotion] and remember [one message]. Total spoken words under 60.

Story opening that hooks

Write the opening 150 words of a [genre] story. Setting: [place]. Main character: [one-line description]. The opening must start in the middle of an event, not with a description, and end on a line that makes the reader ask a question. No backstory in the first paragraph.

Resume bullet polisher

Rewrite these resume bullet points to start with a strong action verb, include a number or result wherever possible, and stay under 20 words each. Remove weak words like "helped", "responsible for" and "worked on". Keep the facts true — do not invent numbers. Bullets: [paste your bullets]
Best for: ChatGPT, Claude

Summarise a long document

Summarise this document in three parts: (1) a 2-sentence overview, (2) the 5 most important points as bullets, each under 15 words, (3) a list of every date, deadline and number mentioned, with no additions. If something is unclear in the document, say so — do not fill the gap. Document: [paste text]
Best for: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini

Explain code in plain English

Explain this code to a beginner who knows no programming. Go line by line, but group related lines together. Use one everyday analogy for the main idea. At the end, say in one sentence what the code achieves and one sentence on what would break if [a specific part] were removed. Code: [paste code]
Best for: ChatGPT, Claude

Debug an error

I am getting this error: [paste the full error message]. Here is the code around it: [paste code]. Tell me: (1) what the error means in plain language, (2) the most likely cause, (3) the smallest fix, with the corrected code, (4) one way to prevent this error next time. Do not rewrite my whole file — change only what is needed.
Best for: ChatGPT, Claude
